---
title: "ExternalDNS Deployment Guide"
description: "Learn how to deploy ExternalDNS to automate DNS record management for Kubernetes ingress controllers, including Deployment-based and DaemonSet-based ingress setups."
---

_ExternalDNS_ automatically manages DNS records for applications exposed through your ingress controller. Configuration depends on whether the ingress controller is deployed as a [Deployment (Option 1)](/guides/clouds/kubernetes-exposing-to-the-internet) or a DaemonSet (Option 2).

**Popular DNS providers:** Cloudflare, Route 53, Google Cloud DNS, DigitalOcean, Vultr, etc.
