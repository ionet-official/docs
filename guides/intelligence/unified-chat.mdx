Unified Chat lets your AI **read, watch,** and **listen** to different types of content in real-time. Once your files are uploaded, the system extracts insights, combines them with contextual knowledge, and generates intelligent, grounded responses to your queries.

Unlike a standard chatbot, Unified Chat is built on top of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), which ensures that every answer is not only relevant but also **traceable back to your own data**.

## What is Unified Chat?

Unified Chat is powered by **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** — a method of pairing a language model with a retrieval system. Instead of depending solely on the model’s pre-trained knowledge, Unified Chat retrieves and integrates relevant content from your uploaded files or connected sources in real-time.

This makes it especially powerful in enterprise, research, and developer workflows, where accuracy, transparency, and traceability are critical.

> 📘 Note: Unified Chat replaces the previous Retrieval Engine. All features from Retrieval Engine are available here, plus new capabilities like multimedia support and creative outputs.
