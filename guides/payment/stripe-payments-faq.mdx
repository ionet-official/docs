You can now use Fiat (via Stripe) to purchase both Compute and IO Credits. This FAQ explains how the payment flow works using Fiat, what options are available, and how Credits are involved.

## General Payment Logic

**Users can pay for compute in two ways:**

- **IO Credits** — internal platform credits.
- **Fiat via Stripe** — credit/debit cards and other Stripe-supported payment methods.

**Minimum amounts:**

- Purchasing IO Credits — **\$1.00**
- Paying directly with Fiat — **\$0.50**

## FAQ

### 1. Can I use Fiat (Stripe) to pay for compute?

Yes. You can now pay for compute directly using Fiat through Stripe. Minimum amount: **\$0.50**

If the amount paid is more than the total cost (cluster cost \+ provider fee) **the remaining balance is added to IO Credits.**

After successful payment, credits are added to the user’s IO Credits balance.

### 3. What happens if I already have enough credits?

If your existing IO Credits balance is enough to cover the compute cost then **Fiat (card) payment is disabled**

A message will appear: `You have sufficient credits to purchase compute. Please proceed to use them.` This ensures credits are used first before showing any fiat option.

Yes. You can extend your compute using Fiat (Stripe). It works the same as initial compute purchase. Minimum fiat amount: **\$0.50**

### 5. How are fees displayed during payment?

When paying through Stripe, you will see:

- **IO Credits Cost / Compute Cost**
- **Payment Provider Fee**
- **Total Amount**

This breakdown makes the payment fully transparent.
